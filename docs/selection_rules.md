# Selection rules for a game night

These are editorial questions from the linked site guides, rewritten as a compact checklist. They are not a formal rating system and have not been experimentally validated.

## Does the game fit the social-play idea?

The site's three-part test asks whether (1) the group can understand a shared job quickly, (2) one player's choice can change another player's moment, and (3) a mistake can become a story the group retells. Price, art budget, horror, and streaming appeal are not required by that definition.

Source: https://bestfriendslopgames.com/what-is-friendslop/

## Will this particular group enjoy it?

Check the official player cap and mode, how the group communicates, the cost of failure or elimination, and whether the game is playable now. A category match alone does not make a title a good fit for every group.

Source: https://bestfriendslopgames.com/what-is-friendslop/

## If everyone joining must fit in one lobby

The five-player guide first requires an official unmodded mode for at least five players. It then considers whether the group can stay meaningfully busy and how quickly it can recover after a failure. The catalog's `official_player_cap` only automates the first, numeric part; it cannot decide role density or recovery speed.

Source: https://bestfriendslopgames.com/best-friendslop-games-for-5-players/

## If the upfront game price must be zero

The free-games guide requires a Free or Free to Play base install, access to the central multiplayer activity without a required payment, and a separate look at setup friction such as accounts, devices, storage, and lobby size. Optional purchases are disclosed rather than treated as a required spend. The guide's store check was dated September 25, 2026; recheck before relying on current pricing.

Source: https://bestfriendslopgames.com/best-free-friendslop-games/

## If the group avoids horror

The non-horror guide excludes games whose core loop depends on being hunted, startled, or trapped in horror ambience. It also identifies the pressure that remains, such as coordination, competition, sabotage, violence, or waiting. The label does not mean a game will be calm or comfortable for everyone.

Source: https://bestfriendslopgames.com/best-friendslop-games-without-horror/
